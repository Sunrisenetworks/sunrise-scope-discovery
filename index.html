Cybersecurity, Infrastructure & Collaboration Discovery Tool

💡 How To Use This Flow

  • Lead with the business problem first, then use the answers to map the right solution set.
  • Each card is written to surface opportunities across cybersecurity, infrastructure, collaboration, backup, network, and physical security.
  • Use the Notes / Context area on the right to capture the client’s own language, timelines, and named systems.
  • When done, tap Save as PDF above to keep a clean working copy.

👋 1. Business Drivers & Stakeholders

Capture the basic account details first, then identify which solution area matters most so the conversation stays commercial from the start. 💬 Try saying: "Before we go deeper, can I capture the company name, your role, and the main area you want help with today?"
This is the key qualification question. It reveals whether the opportunity is a security fix, an infrastructure refresh, a collaboration issue, or a broader transformation project. 💬 Try saying: "What prompted you to speak with us now — a risk, a refresh, a migration, or a service gap?"
A clear view of users, endpoints, sites, servers, and cloud sprawl helps right-size the scope for security and infrastructure work. 💬 Try saying: "Just so I can picture the environment, roughly how many users, devices, sites, and core systems are we talking about?"
This identifies whether the customer is in-house, outsourced, or co-managed, which determines where Sunrise can add immediate value. 💬 Try saying: "Who handles day-to-day support today — your internal team, an MSP, or a mix of both?"
Growth, mergers, site openings, and migrations are the triggers that usually create demand for infrastructure and security work. 💬 Try saying: "What is expected to change over the next 6 to 12 months that we should plan around?"
Early visibility into the buying group prevents a proposal being built for the wrong audience. 💬 Try saying: "Besides you, who else usually needs to weigh in before this can move forward?"

🖥️ 2. Current Environment & Tooling

Understand where production workloads, sensitive data, archives, and backups live so the right security and resilience services can be scoped. 💬 Try saying: "Where do your business applications, data, and backups primarily live today?"
This surfaces Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, mail security, identity gaps, and migration opportunities. 💬 Try saying: "What are you using today for email, collaboration, and identity management?"
This question maps directly to compute, storage, hypervisor, and migration opportunities across the infrastructure portfolio. 💬 Try saying: "What is running your servers, storage, and virtualized workloads today?"
This uncovers branch connectivity, SD-WAN/SASE demand, VPN usage, and remote access gaps. 💬 Try saying: "How do branch offices, remote users, and mobile staff connect back to your network today?"
Map the existing controls to the Sunrise security stack: firewall, EDR, MFA, PAM, DLP, email security, and monitoring. 💬 Try saying: "Which security controls are already running in the environment?"
This makes backup and disaster recovery maturity visible, including restore testing, replication, and target recovery times. 💬 Try saying: "If an outage or ransomware event happened today, how quickly could you restore critical systems?"
This brings the physical layer into scope so CCTV, structured cabling, racks, cooling, and power do not get missed. 💬 Try saying: "Do you have any physical infrastructure, CCTV, or cabling work that should be included alongside the IT scope?"
Understanding recent incidents or outages helps position SOC, email security, PAM, DR, and monitoring correctly. 💬 Try saying: "Has anything happened in the last year or two that changed how you think about risk or uptime?"

⚖️ 3. Risk, Compliance & Continuity

This identifies whether the project is being pushed by regulations, customer requirements, internal governance, or a security framework. 💬 Try saying: "Are there any compliance or governance frameworks shaping what needs to be delivered?"
This surfaces whether the customer needs UAE-only, GCC, or unrestricted hosting for systems and data. 💬 Try saying: "Are there any rules about where your data, backups, or systems must be hosted?"
This is where PAM, vendor access control, and supply-chain risk become visible. 💬 Try saying: "How are vendor, contractor, and privileged administrator accounts controlled today?"
This shows how ready the customer is for outages, attacks, and recovery events across infrastructure and security. 💬 Try saying: "Do you have a tested response plan if something serious disrupts operations?"
Insurance renewals often drive a very practical set of controls, which can become the basis of the proposal. 💬 Try saying: "Do you currently carry cyber insurance, or is that being considered?"

🚀 4. Solution Scope & Commercials

This anchors the conversation to business outcomes so the eventual proposal reflects the customer’s definition of value. 💬 Try saying: "If this goes well, what should be different 12 months from now?"
This is the core portfolio alignment question. It lets Sunrise propose across cybersecurity, infrastructure, collaboration, backup, and physical security in one motion. 💬 Try saying: "Which solution areas should we include in the next round of recommendations or pricing?"
Knowing preferred brands helps with sizing, compatibility, and commercial strategy. 💬 Try saying: "Are there any preferred vendors, or are you looking for a best-fit recommendation regardless of brand?"
Timeline tells you whether this is an urgent fix, a planned refresh, or a longer strategic project. 💬 Try saying: "When do you need this delivered?"
This clarifies who signs off and whether the buying motion needs technical, financial, or executive approval. 💬 Try saying: "Besides the main contact, who needs to approve the final decision?"
Understanding the procurement path keeps the proposal aligned with reality and avoids friction later. 💬 Try saying: "Is this usually a direct award, a shortlist, or a formal tender process?"
This helps frame the proposal at the right level without forcing the customer into an artificial qualification trap. 💬 Try saying: "Is there a working budget range, or should we build the business case first?"
This keeps the form ready for government-style requirements when they are relevant. 💬 Try saying: "Are there any public sector delivery requirements we need to respect?"

🎯 Deep-Dive Solution Tracks

🔒 Deep-Dive: Cybersecurity Scoping

This maps directly to IAM, SSO, MFA, conditional access, and PAM opportunities. 💬 Try saying: "How are user identities, privileged accounts, and access policies managed today?"
Use this to uncover app-layer controls, DLP, email security, CASB, and data classification needs. 💬 Try saying: "Which applications or data sets need the strongest protection?"
This surfaces CSPM, CWPP, cloud misconfiguration review, and cloud workload protection opportunities. 💬 Try saying: "How are cloud environments being monitored and protected right now?"
This shows whether Sunrise can position SOC, SIEM, MDR, logging, or monitoring services. 💬 Try saying: "Who is watching alerts, logs, and threats after hours?"
A direct route into scanning, patching, asset inventory, and ongoing hygiene services. 💬 Try saying: "How do you track vulnerabilities, patches, and asset inventory today?"
This creates a clean segue into awareness training and phishing simulation services. 💬 Try saying: "How are users trained to spot phishing, fraud, and social engineering attacks?"

🌐 Deep-Dive: Network & Infrastructure Scoping

This maps directly to WAN redesign, branch connectivity, SD-WAN, and secure remote access opportunities. 💬 Try saying: "How are branch sites and remote users connected, and do you see a need for SD-WAN or SASE?"
This surfaces switching refreshes, segmentation, PoE, and structured cabling requirements. 💬 Try saying: "What does the switching and cabling layer look like across the sites?"
This uncovers Wi-Fi refreshes, guest portals, rogue AP detection, and controller-managed environments. 💬 Try saying: "How are Wi-Fi, guest access, and wireless security managed today?"
This creates the opening for monitoring, alerting, IPAM, SNMP, NetFlow, and managed network support. 💬 Try saying: "How much visibility do you have into outages, link failures, and network performance?"
This keeps the facilities side of the project visible, including racks, UPS, cooling, and surveillance. 💬 Try saying: "Are there any power, cooling, rack, or CCTV requirements that should be added to the scope?"
